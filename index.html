📖 About ISKCON Lakhimpur

Our Mission

ISKCON Lakhimpur is a spiritual community dedicated to spreading the timeless wisdom of Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagavatam. We welcome everyone to experience the joy of Krishna Consciousness through kirtan, prasadam, and devotional service.

🕉️ Daily Programs

Mangal Aarti (4:30 AM), Bhagavatam Class (7:30 AM), Evening Aarti (7:00 PM), and nightly kirtan.

🍛 Free Prasadam

Daily annadaan serving 200+ devotees. Join us for sanctified vegetarian meals.

📚 Gita Study

Weekly Bhagavad Gita study circles for youth and adults.

🎨 Cultural Events

Janmashtami, Ratha Yatra, Radhashtami, and other grand festivals.
